# Concern: sufficiency

Does the plan follow established patterns and omit nothing the groomed item implies? You are the reviewer for this concern; end your work by recording one verdict for it with record-verdict.

## Questions to ask

1. Does the chosen approach follow the patterns already established in this repo and its cited docs?
2. Does the plan record 2-3 candidate approaches with their trade-offs, and say why one was chosen?
3. Does the plan cover everything the groomed item implies: its acceptance, its verification steps and its dependents?
4. Is the one-item or several-items decision stated, and, for a single item, is the land-bead decision stated?

## Evidence needed to answer ready

- The plan's `design` names the chosen approach and its trade-offs against the alternatives.
- The approach matches an established pattern, or the plan says why it departs from one.
- Every requirement the groomed item implies is addressed by the plan; nothing is silently dropped.

## Verdicts

- ready: all of the evidence above is present.
- gaps: the plan omits something the item implies, or departs from a pattern without a reason. Each gap carries a gap (what is missing) and a research question.
- blocked: the plan cannot be made sufficient without a decision only a person can make. Use block.trigger intent or conflict as fits, and put the question in block.question.
